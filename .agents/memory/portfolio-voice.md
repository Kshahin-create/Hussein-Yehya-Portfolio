name: Portfolio voice
description: The intended personal tone and positioning for Hussein's portfolio copy.
---

Present Hussein as a young student and developer documenting what he learns, builds, joins, and achieves. Keep the language relaxed, direct, confident, and human. Avoid agency language, client acquisition copy, exaggerated expertise, calls to bring business problems, or claims that sound like service marketing.

**Why:** Hussein explicitly wants the portfolio to feel like a free, curious young person recording his journey and achievements—not someone advertising development services.

**How to apply:** Use this voice for all new English, Egyptian Arabic, and Moroccan Arabic copy across the homepage, gallery, projects, achievements, profile, and links.
