---
name: Mobile gallery toolbar
description: Mobile layout rule for the photo-library filter toolbar.
---

On narrow screens, show the gallery result count above the filter pills rather than beside or after them.

**Why:** The filters wrap across several lines, so a side-by-side count becomes cramped and an after-the-filters count may be pushed out of the initial viewport.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy whenever the gallery toolbar or its labels are redesigned for mobile.
